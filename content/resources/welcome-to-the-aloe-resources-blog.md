## Why we started this blog

Running a business in Canada means making decisions with tax and cash-flow
consequences every week. Our goal with these resources is simple: **plain-language
guidance you can actually act on**, without the jargon.

## What to expect

- Short, practical explainers on tax and bookkeeping.
- Deadline reminders and planning checklists.
- Notes on changes that affect owner-managed businesses.

If there's a topic you'd like us to cover, [reach out](/contact) — we read every
message.
