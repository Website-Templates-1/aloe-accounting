## Do you need to register?

Most new businesses can stay unregistered as a **small supplier** until taxable
revenue crosses $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters. After that,
registration is required. Some businesses register voluntarily earlier to claim
input tax credits.

## Filing frequency

The CRA assigns an annual, quarterly, or monthly filing period based on revenue.
Mark the deadlines in your calendar — late filing and remitting can trigger
penalties and interest.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to register after crossing the threshold.
- Not separating collected tax from operating cash.
- Missing input tax credits by losing receipts.
